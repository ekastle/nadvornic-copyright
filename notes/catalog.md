## 2. Circular No. 58 – Music (July 1940)
Explains procedure for copyrighting unpublished musical compositions under Section 11 of the Copyright Act.
- Deposit one complete copy + Form E2 + $1 fee
- Handwritten annotation: "Remitted $2.00 and sent 2 copies in separate covers Apr. 18-1941 for Copyright: 'God Save America'"

## 3. Circular No. 37 – Copyright in Foreign Countries
Explains that U.S. copyright does not automatically protect in European countries (U.S. not then member of Berne Union). Recommends first publication in a Union country.

## 4–5. Application Forms List (Circular No. 12)
Lists forms for books, periodicals, dramas, music (Forms E, E1, E2), etc.

## 6–7. Steps Necessary to Secure Copyright Registration (Circular No. 35)
Detailed instructions under Act of March 4, 1909, as amended. Includes notice requirements and fees ($2 published, $1 unpublished).

## 13–20. Přírodní Léčení (Natural Healing)
Czech-language booklet by Frank Nadvorník, Naturopath, Box 333, Dickinson, N.D.
- Title page with decorative element
- Handwritten note: "Přírodní-means Natural / Léčení-means Healing"

- Approximately 16 pages of dense Czech text.

## Research Notes
- Frank Nadvornik (b. 1877) appears as both a composer/arranger of patriotic and Czech-themed music and a practitioner of naturopathy.
- Addresses: Scio, Oregon (later) and Dickinson, North Dakota (earlier).
- Copyright term under 1909 Act: 28 years + possible 28-year renewal. Most of these works are likely in the public domain if not renewed.
- Further research recommended: Copyright Office renewal records, Czech-American community history, naturopathy in early 20th-century Midwest/Northwest.
