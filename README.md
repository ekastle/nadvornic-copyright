# Nadvornik Copyright Research Collection

Repository for research on historical U.S. Copyright Office documents related to **Frank Nadvornik** (also spelled Nadvorník), a Czech-American naturopath, musician, and composer from Scio, Oregon, and Dickinson, North Dakota.

## Contents

This collection originates from an envelope found while cleaning up a family property. The envelope contained Copyright Office correspondence, registration certificates for musical works, and a Czech-language booklet on natural healing.

### Structure

- `scans/` — Original scanned PDF of the envelope and contents (`Frank_Nadvornik_Copyright.pdf`)
- `transcripts/` — Precise OCR and cleaned transcriptions of the documents, preserving English and Czech text, including handwritten annotations
- `research/` — Per-work research notes and findings (see [research/README.md](research/README.md) for the index)
- `notes/` — General research notes and catalog of works

## Key Documents Identified

1. **Envelope** from Library of Congress Copyright Office to Frank Nadvornik, Scio, Oregon (related to Class E pub. 94647)
2. **Circulars** from Copyright Office (No. 58 on unpublished music, No. 37 on foreign copyright, application forms, steps for registration)
3. **Certificate of Registration**: *God Save America* (words and music by Frank Nadvornik), published May 1, 1941, Entry Class E pub. No. 94647
4. **Certificate of Registration**: *Christmas Chants* (arrangement by Frank Nadvornik), published September 24, 1946
5. **Letter** (January 2, 1934) from Acting Register of Copyrights explaining rights and international copyright
6. **Czech Booklet**: *Přírodní Léčení* (Natural Healing) by Frank Nadvorník, Naturopath, Dickinson, N.D.
7. Additional certificates for Czech marches and compositions (c. 1921–1926), including *Československý Bílý Pochod*, *Česká Družba March*, and *Ave Mundi*

## Languages

- English (official U.S. Copyright Office forms and letters)
- Czech (naturopathy booklet and some musical titles)
- Handwritten cursive annotations in English

## Purpose

This repository is set up for further analysis, including:
- Verifying copyright terms and public domain status
- Transcribing and translating Czech materials
- Researching Frank Nadvornik's life and works
- Cataloging musical compositions

Original scans are retained for reference. Transcriptions aim for precision based on visual review of the scans.
