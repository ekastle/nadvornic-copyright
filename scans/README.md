# Original Scans

The original high-resolution scan of the envelope and its contents is stored in this directory as:

**`Frank_Nadvornik_Copyright.pdf`**

## Document Information

- **Filename:** Frank_Nadvornik_Copyright.pdf
- **Approximate size:** 23 MB
- **Content:** Full color scan of the outer envelope, Copyright Office materials, inner envelope, certificates, and the Czech booklet *Přírodní Léčení*
- **Source:** Found while cleaning a family property after the passing of the owner’s father. The outer envelope contained Copyright Office correspondence and certificates related to Frank Nadvornik; a second (inner) envelope and its contents were also present and scanned.
- **Languages:** English (official forms, certificates, letters) and Czech (naturopathy booklet)
- **Period:** Materials date primarily from 1921 to 1946

## Contents of the Scan

1. Outer envelope from the Library of Congress Copyright Office to Frank Nadvornik, Scio, Oregon (Class E pub. 94647, postmarked January 1941).
2. Copyright Office circulars and instructions (unpublished music, foreign copyright, application forms, registration steps).
3. Certificate of Registration for *God Save America* (words and music by Frank Nadvornik), published 1 May 1941, Entry Class E pub. No. 94647.
4. Certificate for *Christmas Chants* (arrangement by Frank Nadvornik), published 24 September 1946.
5. 1934 letter from the Acting Register of Copyrights.
6. Inner envelope addressed to Frank Nadvornik in Dickinson, North Dakota (c. 1921).
7. Earlier certificates (1921–1926) for Czech-themed musical works, including *Československý Bílý Pochod*, *Česká Družba March*, and *Ave Mundi*.
8. Czech booklet *Přírodní Léčení* (Natural Healing) by Frank Nadvorník, Naturopath, Dickinson, N.D., with handwritten English gloss on the title page.

Cleaned transcriptions of key documents are available in `../transcripts/key_documents.md`.  
A full English translation of the Czech booklet is available in `../transcripts/Prirodni_Leceni_English_Translation.md`.

This scan serves as the primary source document for the research collection.
