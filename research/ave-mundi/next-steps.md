# Next Steps: Obtaining the Score and Further Information

**Goal:** Locate a copy of *Ave Mundi* (words by Joseph Horpesty, music by Frank Nadvornik; published 7 March 1926, Class E, XXc, No. 473682) and related materials.

## 1. Request the deposited copy from the U.S. Copyright Office

1. Use Form Copy or the current digital request process at copyright.gov.
2. Provide these identifiers:
   - Title: Ave Mundi
   - Registration: Class E, XXc, No. 473682
   - Publication date: 7 March 1926
   - Words: Joseph Horpesty
   - Music: Frank Nadvornik
   - Associated place: Dickinson, North Dakota
3. Request a high-resolution PDF or paper copy of the deposit.
4. Note fees and processing times for older deposits.

## 2. Family and personal papers

- Check remaining boxes or music folders from the Dickinson period or later holdings for a score matching this title.
- Look for any correspondence or notes mentioning Joseph Horpesty.

## 3. North Dakota / Dickinson archival searches

- Dickinson Public Library and local historical society
- State Historical Society of North Dakota
- Czech or Bohemian immigrant community records from the mid-1920s
- Search terms: “Frank Nadvornik,” “Ave Mundi,” “Joseph Horpesty,” “Dickinson music”

## 4. Broader Czech-American and sacred music collections

- University special collections holding Czech-American or hymnal materials
- WorldCat, IMSLP, HathiTrust searches for the exact title, registration number, or collaborator name

## 5. Copyright Office catalog follow-up

- Search 1926 Catalog of Copyright Entries (Part 3, Musical Compositions) for confirmation of the entry.
- Check for any later renewals.

## 6. Documentation for this repository

Once obtained, add scans to `scans/` or a `scores/` folder, add a transcription of the text if present, and update the notes here.

**Priority:** Begin with the formal Copyright Office deposit request using the registration number 473682.
