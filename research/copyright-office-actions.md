# Copyright Office Actions

This document consolidates the information needed to request deposit copies of Frank Nadvornik’s registered works from the U.S. Copyright Office. It also serves as a tracking log for submitted requests and results.

**Last updated:** 2026-11-15

## How to use this document

1. Use the details below when completing a request (online system, Form Copy, or email/mail).
2. After submitting a request, record the submission date, method, confirmation number, and any fees in the Tracking table.
3. When materials are received, note the date and add a brief description or link to where the files are stored in this repository (e.g., under `scans/` or a new `scores/` folder).
4. Update the individual work folders under `research/` with findings once copies arrive.

### Request methods (general)

- Online: Search the Copyright Public Records System (https://publicrecords.copyright.gov) and use available request options.
- Form: Form Copy (https://www.copyright.gov/forms/form-copy.pdf) or current equivalent on copyright.gov.
- Contact: Use the “Copies of Works” or “Contact Us” information on copyright.gov. Older deposits may involve retrieval fees and multi-week processing times.

Always include the registration number, title, claimant, and publication date for each work.

- **Publication date:** 14 February 1921

### 4. God Save America

- **Title:** God Save America
- **Words and music:** Frank Nadvornik, of United States
- **Claimant:** Frank Nadvornik, Scio, Oregon

- **Copies received:** 24 April 1941
- **Registration:** Class E pub. No. 94647

- **Notes:** Certificate and outer envelope from the Copyright Office (postmarked January 1941). Handwritten note on Circular No. 58 indicates $2.00 remitted and 2 copies sent April 18, 1941.

### 5. Christmas Chants / Vánoční zpěvy

- **Title (English):** Christmas Chants (also *Christmas chants in both the English and Czech languages*)

- **Type:** Arrangement
- **Publication date:** 24 September 1946
- **Registration:** EP-8701 (Class E)

- **Instrumentation:** SATB chorus, piano, violin 1, violin 2, clarinet, trumpet
- **Notes:** Bilingual arrangement. Certificate appears in the original scan.

## Additional Notes

- All works are registered under the 1909 Copyright Act (Class E for musical compositions).
- Deposit copies from these registrations are the authoritative source for the scores.
- Once materials are received, update the corresponding folder under `research/` and consider adding high-quality scans or PDFs to the repository (using Git LFS if files are large).
- Related circulars and correspondence from the original scan are documented in `transcripts/key_documents.md`.
