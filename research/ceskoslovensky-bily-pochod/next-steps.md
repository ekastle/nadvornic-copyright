# Next Steps: Obtaining the Score and Further Information

**Goal:** Locate a copy of *Československý Bílý Pochod* (Czechoslovak White March) by Frank Nadvornik (published 14 February 1921, Class E, XXc, No. 503142) and related materials.

## 1. Request the deposited copy from the U.S. Copyright Office

1. Use Form Copy or the current digital request process at copyright.gov.
2. Provide these identifiers:
   - Title: Československý Bílý Pochod (Czechoslovak White March)
   - Registration: Class E, XXc, No. 503142
   - Publication date: 14 February 1921
   - Claimant: Frank Nadvornik, Dickinson, North Dakota
   - Copies received: 21 February 1921
3. Request a high-resolution PDF or paper copy of the deposit.
4. Note fees and processing times for older deposits.

## 2. Family and personal papers

- Check remaining boxes or music folders from the Dickinson period or later family holdings for a score matching this title.
- Look for band or community performance materials from early 1920s Czech gatherings.

## 3. North Dakota / Dickinson archival searches

- Dickinson Public Library and local historical society
- State Historical Society of North Dakota
- Czech or Bohemian immigrant community records, fraternal organizations, or local newspapers from 1921
- Search terms: “Frank Nadvornik,” “Bílý Pochod,” “Czechoslovak White March,” “Dickinson music”

## 4. Broader Czech-American collections

- University special collections holding Czech-American materials
- WorldCat, IMSLP, HathiTrust searches for the exact title or registration number

## 5. Copyright Office catalog follow-up

- Search 1921 Catalog of Copyright Entries (Part 3, Musical Compositions) for confirmation of the entry.
- Check for any later renewals.

## 6. Documentation for this repository

Once obtained, add scans to `scans/` or a `scores/` folder, add a lyrics/transcription if applicable, and update the notes here.

**Priority:** Begin with the formal Copyright Office deposit request using the registration number 503142.
