# Československý Bílý Pochod (Czechoslovak White March) (1921)

**Research status:** Initial documentation from family scan; catalog lookup pending.  
**Last updated:** 2026-11-15

## Summary

Musical work registered by Frank Nadvornik while living in Dickinson, North Dakota. One of the earliest copyright certificates found in the family papers, associated with the inner envelope from the early 1920s. Reflects Czech-American patriotic or community music in the period immediately following the founding of Czechoslovakia.

## Key facts (from certificate transcription)

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Title | Československý Bílý Pochod |
| English rendering | Czechoslovak White March |
| Composer / Owner | Frank Nadvornik, Dickinson, N. Dak. |
| Publication date | February 14, 1921 |
| Copies received | February 21, 1921 |
| Registration | Class E, XXc, No. 503142 |
| Register | Thorvald Solberg |
| Place | Dickinson, North Dakota |

## Context

The title and timing align with the wave of Czech and Czechoslovak patriotic music produced by immigrant communities in the United States after World War I and the establishment of the Czechoslovak Republic in 1918. Dickinson, ND, had a significant Czech/Bohemian population at the time.

No digitized score or significant online references to this specific work have been located. The deposited copies remain with the U.S. Copyright Office.
