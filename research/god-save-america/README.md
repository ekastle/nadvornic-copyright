# God Save America (1941)

**Research status:** Initial search complete — score and lyrics not located online.  
**Last updated:** 2026-11-15

## Summary

Original musical composition (words and music) by Frank Nadvornik for mixed voices, registered with the U.S. Copyright Office. Self-claimed publication date of 1 May 1941. This is the work associated with the outer envelope from the Copyright Office found in the family papers (Class E pub. No. 94647).

## Search results

- No digitized score, lyrics, or recording of Nadvornik’s 1941 composition was found in public online sources.
- Several unrelated works share the title “God Save America” (including a hymn by William G. Ballantine set to the Russian Hymn melody, and various later songs). These are distinct from Nadvornik’s registration.
- The deposited copies reside with the U.S. Copyright Office / Library of Congress and do not appear to have been digitized.
