# Christmas Chants (1946)

**Research status:** Initial search complete — score and lyrics not located online.  
**Last updated:** 2026-11-15

## Summary

Bilingual (English and Czech) arrangement of Christmas chants, self-published by Frank Nadvornik in Scio, Oregon. Registered with the U.S. Copyright Office as an arrangement on 24 September 1946.

## Catalog of Copyright Entries entry

From *Catalog of Copyright Entries, New Series, Part 3: Published Musical Compositions, 1946* (Vol. 41):

> **NADVORNIK, FRANK, 1877—**  
> *Christmas chants in both the English and Czech languages* … *Vánoční zpěvy v anglickém a českém jazyku*.  
> Scio, Or., F. Nadvornik. 14 p.  
> © arrangement, 24 Sep 46; publisher; EP-8701.  
>  
> Score: chorus (SATB), piano, violin 1, violin 2, clarinet, and trumpet.

Source: Internet Archive — [catalogofcopyrig413lib](https://archive.org/details/catalogofcopyrig413lib)
