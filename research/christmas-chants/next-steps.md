# Next Steps: Obtaining the Score and Further Information

**Goal:** Locate a copy of the 14-page bilingual score *Christmas Chants* / *Vánoční zpěvy* (arrangement by Frank Nadvornik, 1946, registration EP-8701) and any related family or community materials.

## 1. Request the deposited copy from the U.S. Copyright Office

The official deposit copies from the 1946 registration are the most authoritative source.

### How to request

1. Use the Copyright Office’s online request system or mail/email form for copies of deposited works.
   - Start here: [https://www.copyright.gov/forms/form-copy.pdf](https://www.copyright.gov/forms/form-copy.pdf) (Form Copy) or the current digital equivalent on copyright.gov.
   - Online: Search the Copyright Public Records System and use the “Request Copies” options where available.
2. Provide the following identifiers in the request:
   - Title: *Christmas Chants* (also *Christmas chants in both the English and Czech languages* / *Vánoční zpěvy v anglickém a českém jazyku*)
   - Registration number: **EP-8701**
   - Date of registration / publication: 24 September 1946
   - Claimant / publisher: Frank Nadvornik, Scio, Oregon
   - Class: E (musical composition)
3. Request a high-resolution PDF or paper copy of the deposited score (14 pages).
4. Note any fees (typically charged for retrieval and copying of older deposits) and expected processing time (can be several weeks).

### Contact points

- U.S. Copyright Office, Library of Congress
- Email / phone listed on copyright.gov under “Contact Us” or “Copies of Works”
- Music Division, Library of Congress (for additional context if the deposit was transferred)

## 2. Check family and personal papers

- Review any remaining boxes, music folders, or church-related materials from Frank Nadvornik’s estate or the family property where the original envelope was found.
- Look specifically for a 14-page booklet or score titled *Christmas Chants* or *Vánoční zpěvy*, or loose sheets matching the instrumentation (SATB + piano + violin, clarinet, trumpet).
- Check for correspondence with the Copyright Office after 1946 that might mention distribution of copies.

## 3. Local and regional archival searches (Oregon)

Frank Nadvornik was associated with Scio, Oregon.

- Scio or Albany-area public libraries and historical societies
- Linn County Historical Museum or Oregon Historical Society collections
- Local Catholic or Protestant churches in the Scio / Albany / Salem area that may have used bilingual Czech-English materials
- Czech-American community organizations or fraternal groups active in Oregon in the 1940s

Search terms to use when contacting them: “Frank Nadvornik,” “Christmas Chants,” “Vánoční zpěvy,” “Scio Oregon music.”

## 4. North Dakota / Dickinson connections

Nadvornik also lived and worked in Dickinson, North Dakota (noted on earlier certificates and the naturopathy booklet).

- Dickinson Public Library and local historical society
- State Historical Society of North Dakota
- Czech or Bohemian immigrant community records in the Dickinson area
- Any surviving records of naturopathic or musical activities linked to his name

## 5. Broader Czech-American and music collections

- University or special collections holding Czech-American materials (e.g., University of Nebraska, University of Chicago, or Czech & Slovak Museum & Library)
- Sheet-music databases and catalogs (WorldCat, IMSLP, HathiTrust) using the exact bilingual title or EP-8701
- Interlibrary loan requests once a holding is identified

## 6. Copyright Office catalog follow-up

- Confirm whether a renewal was filed (relevant for determining current public-domain status under the 1909 Act).
- Search the Catalog of Copyright Entries for later years (especially 1973–1974) for any renewal of EP-8701.
- Check the Copyright Public Records System (copyright.gov) for the registration and any subsequent records.

## 7. Documentation for this repository

Once a copy is obtained:

1. Add a high-quality scan or PDF of the score to `scans/` (or a new `scores/` folder if preferred).
2. Create a cleaned transcription of the lyrics (English and Czech) in `transcripts/`.
3. Update this file and the main `research/christmas-chants/README.md` with findings.
4. Note instrumentation details, any performance notes, and copyright status.
